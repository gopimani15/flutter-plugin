## 0.1.2

* `TryOnConfig.eyeRotationMm` (default 11) and `TryOnConfig.cameraDiagonalFovDeg` (default 75), web only: tune or turn off (`eyeRotationMm: 0`) the near-to-distance PD correction of eyewear_tryon_web 0.1.2.

## 0.1.1

* `FrameSpec.showTemples` (default `true`): `false` draws the frame front only, with no temple arms.
* `FrameSpec.removePhotoTemples` (default `true`): clears the temple arms a front photo shows through its lenses.

## 0.1.0

* Initial release: `FrameSpec`, `FaceMeasurements`, `TryOnConfig`, events and `GlassesTryOnPlatform` with a method-channel implementation.
